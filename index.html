Our Philosophy
Bridging the gap between teachers and students.
iOT was born from a single observation — there is a quiet but profound communication gap between teachers and the students they serve. We exist to close that gap, and to build something more important than marks.
We don't only teach subjects. We build confidence, sharpen communication, instill discipline, and introduce students to AI-powered learning — preparing the next generation of intelligent Indian youth.

A Letter from the Founder
"Education is evolving. We choose to lead it."
I started iOT because I saw bright students held back not by talent — but by silence. By the inability to ask, to speak, to believe. We're building a different kind of classroom: one where curiosity is loud, discipline is shared, and technology is a tool for thinking — not a shortcut around it.
— Amaan Rizvi
